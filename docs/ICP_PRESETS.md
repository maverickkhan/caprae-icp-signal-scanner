# ICP presets (seeded into icp_profiles)

## 1. Search-fund buy-box
Established, owner-operated service businesses that could be acquired from a retiring founder. Ideally in business for 20+ years, family-run or founder-led with the owner still visibly involved, a small team (roughly 5–50 people), serving a local or regional market, and with no obvious succession plan. Signs of an under-invested digital presence (dated website, old copyright year, no careers page) are a plus because they point to operational upside after acquisition. Avoid franchises, private-equity-backed companies, and businesses that are clearly part of a larger group.

## 2. B2B sales ICP
Growing small-to-mid-sized companies that are likely to buy B2B software or services now. Look for active hiring (careers page with open roles), multiple locations or recent expansion, a modern web stack or online booking/quoting, a clearly named decision-maker (owner, operations or IT lead) with public contact details, and signs of recent investment such as new services, certifications or awards. Deprioritize companies with no web presence, single-person operations, or signs of decline.

> Edit these before starting the clock if you want different wording. The app compiles each description into 4–8 weighted criteria that users can tweak.
